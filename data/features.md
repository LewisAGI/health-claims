# Suggested features: health-claims site
- Claim cards filterable by person, topic and rating, with an evidence-type badge (RCT / SR / cohort / animal / mechanistic).
- "Verified quote" vs "paraphrase" label, linking to the transcript and timestamp where available.
- Changelog per claim (ratings change when new trials land) and a "last reviewed" date.
- Evidence pyramid explainer, plus "association vs causation" primer.
- Right-of-reply / corrections submission form (neutral-tone policy page).
- Sponsorship/conflict-of-interest context field, kept factual.
